## Bug Fixes

- In the All Users view, the clear-completed confirmation now says it deletes every user's completed reminders.
- Moving a completed reminder to another owner keeps it completed.
- Moving a dated reminder to another owner while due dates are switched off now creates a Calendaria note for the new owner.
- Renaming a dated reminder while due dates are switched off now renames its Calendaria note.
- The reminder created, completed and deleted hooks now fire for users added after the world loaded.
